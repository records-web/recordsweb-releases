# RecordsWeb 5.1.1 — Public homepage service presentation

RecordsWeb 5.1.1 updates the public homepage to match the Version 5 multi-service platform.

## Changes

- Fixes Clinical / Policing / Complete product cards appearing white in public dark mode.
- Fixes the community-logo chooser appearing light/white in public dark mode.
- Replaces the clinical-only feature list with platform-wide operational capabilities.
- Presents Primary Care (GP), Secondary Care (Hospital), Ambulance / PHEM and Policing as supported service types.
- Labels each service with its RecordsWeb Clinical or RecordsWeb Policing product.

No database migration or Edge Function redeployment is required for 5.1.1.
