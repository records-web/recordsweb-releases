# RecordsWeb 4.0.3 — Legal Policies

Version 4.0.3 publishes the RecordsWeb Privacy Policy and Terms of Service directly inside both the Website and Electron distributions.

## Included

- Public `#/privacy` Privacy Policy page.
- Public `#/terms` Terms of Service page.
- RecordsWeb roleplay/simulation disclaimer displayed prominently on both policy pages.
- Privacy and Terms links added to public site footers.
- Privacy and Terms links added to the staff login screen.
- Access-request form now requires acknowledgement of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before submission.
- Policies cover RecordsWeb 4.x session monitoring, platform moderation, IP/network restrictions, hashed Electron device identifiers, browser installation identifiers, Discord/Roblox integrations, Shared Care, billing, support sessions and break-glass auditing.

No database migration or Edge Function redeployment is required for this release.
