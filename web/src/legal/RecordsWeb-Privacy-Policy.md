# RecordsWeb Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026  
**Last updated:** 17 September 2026  
**Policy version:** 1.0

## 1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RecordsWeb ("RecordsWeb", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the RecordsWeb website, desktop/Electron application, platform-management services, Discord integration, Roblox integration, APIs, support services and related features.

RecordsWeb is a **roleplay and simulation platform**. It is not an NHS service, healthcare provider, clinical system, electronic health record, medical device or emergency service.

**Do not enter real patient records, real medical information, real prescriptions, real NHS numbers, or other real-world special-category health information into RecordsWeb.** Roleplay patient records should be fictional or otherwise created solely for simulation.

For privacy questions or requests, contact:

**Email:** contactus@recordsweb.org


## 2. Who is responsible for your information

RecordsWeb is responsible for personal information used to operate user accounts, authentication, platform security, moderation, support, billing, service administration and platform-wide integrations.

A community or organisation using RecordsWeb may separately decide what information it creates or enters about its own members. Where that information is personal information and the community independently determines why and how it is used, that community may have its own data-protection responsibilities.

RecordsWeb does not authorise communities to use the platform as a real clinical-record system or to upload genuine patient medical records.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Account and profile information

We may process information such as your name or display name, email address, RecordsWeb username, title, assigned role or roles, organisation/community membership, account status and user ID.

### 3.2 Organisation and community information

We may process organisation names, organisation codes, workspace configuration, staff roles, permissions, community branding, billing status, integration settings and other administrative information supplied by authorised community administrators.

### 3.3 Roleplay and simulation content

RecordsWeb may store fictional patient profiles, simulated consultations, medication entries, fit notes, documents, appointments, referrals, investigations, shared-care records and other roleplay content created by users.

This content must not be used for genuine healthcare or contain real patient medical records.

Some roleplay profiles may include identifiers connected to a real platform account, such as a Discord user ID or Roblox user ID, so that roleplay features can function. Those identifiers are personal information even where the medical content itself is fictional.

### 3.4 Discord and Roblox information

If you use an integration, we may process information such as Discord user ID, Discord verification status, delivery status for RecordsWeb messages, Roblox user ID, Roblox username, Roblox universe/place information, game-connection information, linked roleplay-patient profiles and integration activity.

RecordsWeb does not require your Discord password or Roblox password.

### 3.5 Technical, security and session information

To protect RecordsWeb, we may process IP address, approximate network location derived from IP, session identifiers, login and logout times, failed login events, browser/app type, operating system, RecordsWeb version, device type, session status, security events, audit events, moderation actions and timestamps.

For the Electron application, RecordsWeb may create a **one-way SHA-256 hash of a device/machine identifier** for session security and device restrictions. The raw machine identifier is intended to remain on the device and is not stored by RecordsWeb.

For website sessions, RecordsWeb may use a locally generated installation identifier to distinguish browser installations for security purposes.

### 3.6 Security and moderation information

If security or moderation controls are used, we may store account restrictions, IP or network restrictions, device-hash restrictions, restriction scope, expiry, moderation reasons, internal moderation notes, appeal/review information, revoked-session records, break-glass access events and platform-support-session records.

### 3.7 Billing information

Where paid RecordsWeb services are offered, payments may be processed by Stripe or another payment provider. RecordsWeb may receive transaction identifiers, subscription status, billing status, plan information, payment outcome and limited billing details.

RecordsWeb should not receive or store full payment-card numbers where the payment provider handles those details directly.

### 3.8 Support and communications

If you contact RecordsWeb, we may process your email address, name, account information, support request, attachments, correspondence and technical information needed to investigate your request.

## 4. How we use information

We use personal information to provide and authenticate RecordsWeb accounts; operate community workspaces and permissions; provide website, Electron, Discord, Roblox and API features; maintain sessions; deliver roleplay documents or notifications where requested; process billing; answer support requests; prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access; investigate security incidents; enforce platform restrictions; maintain audit records; diagnose faults; maintain and improve reliability; and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use roleplay medical content to provide real medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

## 5. Lawful bases

Where UK data-protection law applies, RecordsWeb relies on an appropriate lawful basis for each use of personal information.

We may rely on **contract** where processing is necessary to provide an account or service you requested; **legitimate interests** for proportionate security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, service administration, audit logging and platform protection; **legal obligation** where information must be retained or disclosed by law; and **consent** where consent is the appropriate basis, for example for certain optional features or non-essential technologies.

Where legitimate interests are used, RecordsWeb must consider the necessity of the processing and its impact on users.

RecordsWeb is not intended to process genuine health information or other real special-category patient data as part of its roleplay records.

## 6. Security monitoring, device identifiers and bans

RecordsWeb uses security controls designed to prevent account compromise and platform abuse.

These controls may include login rate limits, session monitoring, account restrictions, IP/network restrictions, hashed-device restrictions, audit logs, step-up authentication, security PINs and manual moderation.

An authorised RecordsWeb platform moderator may inspect session metadata across RecordsWeb for security and moderation purposes. This does **not** include access to users' passwords or raw authentication tokens.

Automated security checks may immediately refuse access when an active account, IP/network or device restriction is detected. Significant or permanent moderation actions should be reviewable by an authorised moderator. You may contact **contactus@recordsweb.org** if you believe a restriction was applied incorrectly.

## 7. Break-glass and support access

Certain RecordsWeb features may allow time-limited emergency or support access to otherwise restricted information.

Where used, RecordsWeb may record the authorised user, reason, affected organisation or roleplay record, start time, expiry, actions taken and related audit information.

Such access is intended to be visible in security logs and subject to review.

## 8. Sharing of information

We may share or make information available to service providers only where needed to operate RecordsWeb. Depending on the feature, these may include Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare, GitHub, Discord, Roblox, Stripe and communications or support infrastructure used by the RecordsWeb internal team.

Each third-party service processes information under its own terms and privacy arrangements.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the platform, investigate abuse, establish or defend legal claims, or respond to valid legal requests.

**RecordsWeb does not sell personal information.**

## 9. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, RecordsWeb will use an appropriate transfer mechanism or rely on another lawful basis for the transfer.

## 10. Retention

RecordsWeb keeps information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including account operation, security, moderation, dispute handling, backups and legal obligations.

Account and workspace information may be retained while an account or organisation remains active. Security, audit and moderation records may be retained after account closure where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, preserve the integrity of security controls, enforce restrictions or resolve disputes.

Backups and provider logs may persist for a limited period after deletion before being overwritten or expiring under infrastructure-provider retention schedules.


## 11. Cookies and local storage

RecordsWeb may use cookies or browser/local storage that are necessary for authentication, security, preferences, session continuity and installation identification.

If RecordsWeb introduces non-essential analytics, advertising or similar technologies that require consent, users will be given the required information and choices before those technologies are used.

## 12. Data security

RecordsWeb uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information. These may include role-based access, server-side permission checks, row-level database security, hashed device identifiers, security PINs, session revocation, audit logging, restricted-record controls, time-limited support access, rate limiting and access monitoring.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials secure and reporting suspected compromise promptly.

## 13. Young users

RecordsWeb may be used alongside online services that are popular with younger users, including Roblox and Discord.

RecordsWeb should not knowingly use children's information in ways that are unnecessary for the service. Where the service is likely to be accessed by users under 18, privacy information and settings should be designed with age-appropriate protections in mind.

Users must also meet any minimum-age requirements imposed by Discord, Roblox or other third-party services they choose to connect.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact **contactus@recordsweb.org**.

## 14. Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to certain processing, portability of information you provided, and withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

Some rights are subject to legal exceptions. For example, RecordsWeb may need to retain limited security or moderation information where deletion would undermine fraud or abuse prevention, provided there is a lawful basis to do so.

To make a request, email **contactus@recordsweb.org** and clearly identify the account involved and the right you wish to exercise. RecordsWeb may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

## 15. Complaints

If you have concerns about how RecordsWeb uses your personal information, contact **contactus@recordsweb.org** first so the issue can be investigated.

Where UK data-protection law applies, you may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

## 16. Third-party services

RecordsWeb integrations with Discord, Roblox, Stripe, GitHub or other services are also subject to those providers' own terms and privacy policies.

RecordsWeb is not responsible for independent processing performed by those third parties outside RecordsWeb's control.

## 17. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when RecordsWeb features, security controls, service providers or legal requirements change.

Material changes should be communicated through the website, application, platform notice or another appropriate method. The current version and effective date will be shown at the top of the policy.
